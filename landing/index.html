Questions

Tower, requesting clarification.

FREQ 121.5 · STANDING BY
FP-01Does FinPilot connect to my bank or marketplace?
TWR

Not today. You enter the cash you’ve confirmed — on purpose, so the forecast starts from a number you trust. Spreadsheet import (Excel and CSV) is next on the flight plan.

FP-02Is this accounting software?
TWR

No. It won’t do bookkeeping or taxes, and it never moves money. It answers one forward-looking question: will the cash be there on the day each bill is due?

FP-03Why stop at 56 days?
TWR

Eight weeks catches two payroll cycles and two rent dates. Further out, guesses start to outnumber facts, so the forecast stops where it can still be honest.

FP-04Which currency does it use?
TWR

Vietnamese đồng, in whole numbers. No decimals to invent and no rounding to hide — ₫50,000,001 stays exactly that.

FP-05What happens when the line dips below zero?
TWR

FinPilot names the first day you’d be short, by how much, and which bill is due — while there’s still time to delay, split or resize the order.
